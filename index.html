Our Story

Textiles with a sense of place.

La Maison d’Afrique is a contemporary African luxury textile house. We create, curate, source and develop expressive textiles, combining heritage weaving, artisanal hand-dyeing and bespoke development.

Our ambition is to bring African textiles into contemporary fashion and interiors, with thoughtful curation and respect for the hands that make them.
